Predictive maintenance with NASA C-MAPSS

Bachelor’s thesis

July 2026

Grade: 1.3

The challenge

Predict the remaining useful life (RUL) of simulated aircraft engines from multivariate sensor data, to support better maintenance decisions.

What I did

  • Built a reproducible preprocessing and feature engineering pipeline with 30-cycle sliding windows and 102 statistical features.
  • Compared an age-based baseline with Random Forest, XGBoost and a GRU neural network on the C-MAPSS FD001 dataset.
  • Used SHAP to explain predictions and identify the most important sensors.
  • Added conformal prediction to quantify predictive uncertainty.
  • Simulated maintenance decisions based on the predicted RUL.

Results

XGBoost performed best.

10.10MAE, in cycles
13.41RMSE, in cycles
93%test coverage at a 90% target

Formula 1 pit stop prediction

Seminar paper

December 2025

Grade: 1.7

The challenge

Use historical Formula 1 race data to predict whether a driver will pit on the next lap.

What I did

  • Collected lap-by-lap data from the 2024 season with FastF1 and processed it with Pandas.
  • Engineered features such as tire age, tire compound, lap times, race position, weather and track status.
  • Compared Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, XGBoost and LSTM networks.
  • Tested how much historical race context improves the predictions.

Findings

Sequential race information improves pit stop prediction, and the LSTM benefited most from that context. The models are a starting point for simulating and optimizing pit stop strategy.
