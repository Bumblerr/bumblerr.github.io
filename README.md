## Featured Projects

### 01 — Predictive Maintenance with NASA C-MAPSS

*Bachelor's Thesis | July 2026 | Grade: 1.3*

**The Challenge**

Predict the *Remaining Useful Life (RUL)* of simulated aircraft engines using multivariate sensor data to support more effective maintenance decisions.

**What I Did**

- Developed a reproducible data preprocessing and feature engineering pipeline using 30-cycle sliding windows and 102 statistical features.
- Compared an age-based baseline with **Random Forest, XGBoost, and a GRU neural network** on NASA's C-MAPSS FD001 dataset.
- Applied **SHAP** to explain model predictions and identify important sensor features.
- Implemented **conformal prediction** to quantify predictive uncertainty.
- Simulated maintenance decisions based on predicted remaining useful life.

**Key Results**

- Best-performing model: **XGBoost**
- Mean Absolute Error (MAE): **10.10 cycles**
- Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE): **13.41 cycles**
- Conformal prediction: **93% empirical test coverage** at a nominal 90% coverage level.

**Technologies**

`Python` `Pandas` `Scikit-learn` `XGBoost` `GRU` `SHAP` `Machine Learning`

### 02 - Formula 1 Pit Stop Decision Modeling

*Seminar Paper | December 2025 | Grade: 1.7*

**The Challenge**

Use historical Formula 1 race data to predict whether a driver will make a pit stop on the next lap.

**What I Did**

- Collected and processed lap-by-lap data from the **2024 Formula 1 season** using FastF1 and Pandas.
- Engineered features including tire age, tire compound, lap times, race position, weather conditions, and track status.
- Implemented and compared **Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, XGBoost, and LSTM neural networks**.
- Investigated how historical race context influences prediction performance.

**Key Insights**

- Sequential race information improves pit stop prediction.
- LSTM models demonstrated strong predictive capabilities by incorporating historical race context.
- The models provide a foundation for future simulation and optimization of Formula 1 pit stop strategies.

**Technologies**

`Python` `Pandas` `FastF1` `XGBoost` `LSTM` `Deep Learning` `Time-Series Analysis`
